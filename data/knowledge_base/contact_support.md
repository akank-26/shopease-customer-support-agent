
# ShopEase Customer Support

Document ID: SUPPORT-001
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: 2026-11-01

## Human Support

Customers can request human assistance through
the ShopEase support handoff process.

In this prototype, escalation is simulated.
No real support ticket is created.

## Escalation Scenarios

Human support is required for:

- Refund approvals and execution
- Order cancellations
- Returns requiring manual review
- Unresolved delivery issues
- Cases where required information is unavailable

## Contact Information

No verified public support email address,
phone number, or website URL is provided
in this fictional knowledge base.

The AI assistant must not invent contact details.

## Agent Limitations

The AI assistant cannot directly modify orders,
issue refunds, or cancel purchases.
