
# ShopEase Shipping Policy

Document ID: SHIPPING-001
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: 2026-11-01

## Standard Shipping

Standard shipping typically takes 3–5 business
days after dispatch.

## Express Shipping

Express shipping typically takes 1–2 business
days after dispatch.

## Delivery Estimates

Delivery timelines are estimates, not guarantees.

Actual delivery status must be obtained from
the order tracking system.

## Delayed Deliveries

Customers should contact support when an order
is delayed beyond the estimated delivery window.

## Delivery Exceptions

Delivery exceptions may include:

- Incorrect delivery address
- Failed delivery attempt
- Carrier disruption
- Customs delay

An active delivery exception may affect refund
eligibility under the refund policy.
