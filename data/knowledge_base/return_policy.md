
# ShopEase Return Policy

Document ID: RETURN-001
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: 2026-11-01

## Return Window

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar
days of the delivery date.

## Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for a return:

- The item must be unused.
- The item must be in its original packaging.
- The customer must provide a valid order ID.

## Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

- Digital products
- Personalized products
- Gift cards

## Electronics

Headphones and other electronics are returnable
within 30 days if unused and in their original
packaging.

## After 30 Days

Return requests submitted after 30 days require
human support review. Approval is not guaranteed.
