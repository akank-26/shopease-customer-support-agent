
# ShopEase Refund Policy

Document ID: REFUND-001
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: 2026-11-01

## Approved Returns

Refunds for approved returns are issued to the
original payment method.

Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days
after the returned item passes inspection.

## Undelivered Orders

An undelivered order may qualify for a refund when:

1. At least 10 calendar days have passed since shipment.
2. The order has not been delivered.
3. There is no active delivery exception.

All three conditions must be verified before
refund eligibility can be confirmed.

## Refund Execution

Only authorized human support representatives
can approve and initiate refunds.

The AI assistant cannot issue refunds.

## Missing Information

If eligibility cannot be established from available
order information, refer the customer to human support.
